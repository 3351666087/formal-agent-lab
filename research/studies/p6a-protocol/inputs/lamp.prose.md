A lamp is off and its toggle counter is 0. The counter can only hold the values 0 to 3.

Actions:
- toggle — switches the lamp (off to on, on to off) and adds 1 to the counter. It cannot be done when the counter is already 3.
- reset — sets the counter to 0 and switches the lamp off. It can only be done when the counter is above 0.

Goal three_toggles: the counter equals 3.
